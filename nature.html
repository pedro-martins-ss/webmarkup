Walking routes

Try Rondje Konijnenberg from Koevlak: a 3.5 km loop with soft sand and a short, steep climb. The card below has the start and route details.

Cycling outdoors

Use the marked cycle paths in Zuid-Kennemerland. Do not cycle on walking paths. Check entrance signs for local rules.

Check cycling rules ↗

Sport and movement

Running on the paths is a popular way to explore. Group activities can need permission, and rules differ between parts of the park, so check the signs at each entrance.

Tip: the rules are on the sign at each entrance.

Look after the area

Stay on marked paths, take your litter home and check signs about dogs and protected areas. The park is open from sunrise to sunset.

Read the park rules ↗

Route card

First walk: Rondje Konijnenberg

Start at Koevlak and follow the green arrows. The loop has soft sand and a steep climb. Check the park's route page before you go.

Distance 3.5 km
Time 1 hour 15 minutes
Start Koevlak entrance, Zeeweg (next to the visitor centre)
Marked with green arrows

Before you go

Prepare for the route

Check the weather, pack water and dress for the route. Tell someone where you're going on a longer walk.
