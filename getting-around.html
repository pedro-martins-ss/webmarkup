Getting around

Getting around Overveen

Overveen station is one stop from Haarlem. Here are the basics for using the train, bus and bike.

Check your route before leaving

Choose how you travel

Train

Tip: how to pay

Tap in before boarding and tap out with the same contactless card or device. A missed check-out can cost extra. Check that your card is supported.

How to pay for the train on ns.nl ↗

Bus

Buses link Overveen with Haarlem and nearby villages. Check the line number and direction at the stop. You can pay on board with a contactless bank card.

Plan a bus trip on 9292.nl ↗

Cycling and walking

Most short trips are easiest by bike. Use front and rear lights after dark, ride on the red cycle paths and give way at "shark teeth" road markings (▽▽▽).

Tip: lock your bike with two locks.

Parking basics

Use the bike racks at the station and keep railings clear. Driving? Check the signs, as some streets need a parking permit.
