Events and activities

Look for weekly or monthly activities. Ask which language is used, what it costs and if you can try it once.

Tip: try a regular activity.

Clubs and local groups

Ask a sports, music or hobby club about a trial session, the fee and whether someone can help you in English.

Tip: ask if you can try a session first.

Meeting places

Try a local café, club canteen or meeting place. Say hello and introduce yourself. A simple hallo is a good start.

Tip: say "hallo" and introduce yourself.

Helping locally

Welzijn Bloemendaal lists volunteer roles across the municipality. Volunteering can help you meet neighbours and practise Dutch.

Tip: ask Welzijn Bloemendaal about current roles.

Weekly rhythm

An example week for a newcomer

Regular activities let you meet the same people again. This plan is just an example. Check real days and times with the organiser.

  • MON Club training Many sports clubs train on weekday evenings.
  • TUE Rest day Leave an evening free.
  • WED Volunteer shift Find a role through Welzijn Bloemendaal.
  • THU Dutch practice Practise with a neighbour or a conversation group.
  • FRI Club canteen Stay for a drink after training.
  • SAT Market in Haarlem Market day on the Grote Markt, one train stop away.
  • SUN Dune walk Try the green route from the Koevlak entrance.
