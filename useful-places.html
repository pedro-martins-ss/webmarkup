Daily needs

The village centre has shops for everyday needs. Check the shop's opening times before your first visit, especially on Sundays.

Tip: check Sunday opening times.

Health care: find a GP

In the Dutch finder, choose huisarts and enter Overveen or your postcode. Ask if a practice takes new patients. Have your ID, BSN and insurance details ready. Your insurer can help if you get stuck.

Your first month

Four steps to get settled

Start with address registration. Other steps can overlap; check the official rules for your situation.

  1. 1 On arrival

    Register your address

    If you will stay over 4 months, register with your municipality within 5 days of arrival. Book an appointment with Bloemendaal.

  2. 2 With your BSN

    Request your DigiD

    Apply using your BSN, registered address and mobile phone. DigiD is your login for online government services.

  3. 3 Check promptly

    Arrange health insurance

    Check whether you need Dutch basic health insurance. If it applies to you, arrange cover within 4 months of arrival.

    Check insurance rules ↗
  4. 4 Once you settle

    Register with a GP

    Find a practice via Health care above. Ask about postcode coverage and new patients.
